# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and respectful environment for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy and respect.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Focus disagreements on ideas and outcomes, not people.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, and other abusive behavior are not
acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner. Reports will be
reviewed promptly, fairly, and confidentially. The repository owner may remove
content or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to
protect the community.
